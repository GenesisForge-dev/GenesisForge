Keep your family together.

Kinvale is a family-tree app for Android. Build your tree, keep the memories and the dates that matter, and see exactly how everyone is related.

Coming soon to Google Play

What you can do

Your tree, your waySeveral views of the tree, from a simple family line to everyone in one picture.
Relationship namesFind out how two people are related, with names in English, Telugu and Hindi.
MemoriesAttach photos, voice notes and stories to people.
Dates and remindersBirthdays, anniversaries and events in one calendar, with reminders.
Basic mode for eldersA large, simple screen with just the calendar, reminders and the family.
Family quizPlay together on one phone and see how well you know your family.
Family bookMake a printable PDF book with a page for each person.
Share and back upShare parts of the tree with relatives, or save a backup file.

Private by design

There is no account and no sign-up. Your family tree is stored on your phone, encrypted, and is not uploaded to our servers. You can lock the app with your fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern. Read the privacy policy.
