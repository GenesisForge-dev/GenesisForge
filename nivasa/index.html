Everything your home runs on

₹

Money in and out

Every expense and every rupee received, by category and account. Monthly budget with "₹ left per day". Speak "milk 60" or scan a shop bill.

💳

Accounts and credit cards

Cash, bank, wallets and cards with live balances. See each card's available limit and outstanding, and get reminded before the due date.

🌿

Grocery stock and prices

One tap saves stock, price per kg and the expense. Know when rice runs out, which shop is cheapest, and how much more your basket costs now.

🥛

Deliveries and household help

Mark the daily milk or water can in one tap, track household help's days and advances, and settle the month without arguments.

🔔

Subscriptions and bills

OTT, EMIs, SIPs, premiums, rent and electricity. Auto-debits are booked by themselves; Nivasa even spots regular payments for you.

✨

Smart insights

Month-end forecast, "higher than usual" alerts and categories that learn your shops – all worked out on the phone.

Private by design

🔒 No sign-up and no server: your entries stay in the app's private storage on your phone.
🛡️ Your money details are never shared with anyone, including advertisers.
👆 Optional app lock with your fingerprint, face or phone PIN.
💾 Back up to a file you keep (Google Drive, WhatsApp to yourself) and restore it on a new phone.
